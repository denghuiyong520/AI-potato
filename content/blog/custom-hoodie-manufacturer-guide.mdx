Hoodies are one of the highest-margin and most reordered items in the apparel market. For streetwear brands, athleisure labels, and DTC clothing businesses, a great custom hoodie is often the anchor product. But sourcing one from the right manufacturer requires knowing what to look for — from fleece weight to construction details to decoration methods.

This guide walks you through everything you need to know about finding and working with a custom hoodie manufacturer.

## The Hoodie Market Opportunity

The global hoodie and sweatshirt market was valued at over $40 billion in 2023 and continues to grow. Key demand drivers:
- Streetwear culture driving premium construction expectations
- Athleisure blending casual and performance needs
- DTC brand founders launching with hoodies as hero SKUs
- Corporate and promotional apparel demand

For a brand founder, a well-sourced hoodie at 350+ gsm with a clean, fitted silhouette and quality screen print can retail for $65–$120. With a manufacturing cost of $18–$30 and modest shipping, the margins work — but only with the right manufacturing partner.

## Fleece Fabric: The Most Important Decision

The fleece weight and construction determines everything about your hoodie. Here's what you need to know:

### GSM (Grams per Square Metre)

| GSM Range | Feel | Best For |
|-----------|------|----------|
| 240–280 gsm | Lightweight, drapey | Spring/summer, athleisure |
| 300–340 gsm | Mid-weight, standard | Year-round basics |
| 350–400 gsm | Heavyweight, premium | Streetwear, luxury basics |
| 400–500 gsm | Ultra-heavyweight | Cold climate, premium tier |

Most successful streetwear brands use 380–420 gsm.

### Fleece Construction Types

**French Terry** — Loop-back construction. Smooth outside, looped inside. Lighter and more drapey. Popular for joggers and cropped styles.

**Brushed Fleece** — The loops are brushed into a soft nap. Warmer, softer feel. Standard for pullover hoodies.

**Unbrushed Fleece** — Raw loop interior. More casual, slightly less warm. Used in athletic and transitional styles.

**Sherpa-lined** — Fleece exterior with sherpa lining for premium warmth. Higher cost but justifies higher retail.

### Yarn Content

- **100% Cotton** — Natural feel, slightly heavier, less stretch recovery
- **80/20 Cotton/Polyester** — Better shape retention, slightly smoother hand
- **70/30 Cotton/Polyester** — Budget option, common in promotional apparel
- **100% Organic Cotton** — Premium sustainable option, GOTS certified
- **Recycled Fleece (rPET)** — Growing demand in sustainable brands

## Hoodie Styles and Silhouettes

Before approaching a manufacturer, clarify your silhouette:

**Pullover hoodie** — Classic. Kangaroo pocket or no pocket. Double-layered hood or single layer.

**Zip-up hoodie** — Full zip or half zip. Requires additional zipper sourcing (request YKK for premium).

**Cropped hoodie** — Shorter body length, popular in women's and unisex streetwear.

**Oversized hoodie** — Dropped shoulders, wide body. Requires adjusted pattern cutting — confirm the manufacturer has done this construction before.

**Coach jacket / windbreaker hybrid** — Light shell construction with hoodie styling. Very different manufacturing process.

## Construction Details That Separate Good from Great

When briefing a manufacturer, specify:

- **Hood type**: Single-layer, double-layer, or structured with wiring
- **Drawstring**: Flat, round, with or without metal aglets
- **Cuffs and waistband**: Custom ribbing weight (150–250 gsm), 1×1 or 2×2 rib
- **Seam type**: Flatlock (athletic) vs. standard overlock
- **Pocket**: No pocket, kangaroo, zip pocket, side seam pockets
- **Label**: Tear-away, woven replacement, printed interior

## Decoration Options for Custom Hoodies

### Screen Printing

Best for bold graphics, simple designs, and solid colour fills. The most common choice for streetwear brands at volumes of 50+ units. Multiple colour separations are possible; cost rises with colour count.

**Pros:** Vivid, durable, cost-effective at volume  
**Cons:** Setup fees per colour, minimum run sizes for efficiency

### Embroidery

Structured thread application. Ideal for logos, text, badges. Adds a premium, tactile quality that's highly valued at retail.

Across [our manufacturing network](/factory), we run multi-head embroidery with up to 15-colour thread capability, and digitising is included with your order.

### DTG (Direct-to-Garment)

Full-colour, photo-quality printing directly onto the fabric. No setup fees. Best for complex gradients or artwork. Less durable than screen printing for simple designs.

### Puff Printing

A raised, 3D texture achieved with expanding ink. Very popular for streetwear logos. Creates a premium look at low cost per unit.

### Heat Transfer / DTF

Versatile for complex designs, small orders, and detailed artwork. DTF (Direct to Film) is increasingly popular as a screen printing alternative.

## What MOQ Should You Expect?

For custom cut & sew hoodies from a professional manufacturer:

- **Logo customisation on existing blank styles:** MOQ 50 pieces per colour
- **Fully custom cut & sew (your pattern, your fabric):** MOQ 100 pieces per style per colour

## Sampling: Never Skip This Step

A custom hoodie has significantly more variables than a t-shirt — fleece weight, ribbing, drawstring, print registration, and silhouette all need to be verified before bulk production.

Standard sample timeline: **10–15 business days** for a fully custom hoodie prototype.

What to check in your sample:
- Fleece weight (request a fabric swatch and weigh it)
- Seam construction and stitch density
- Hood shape and proportion
- Fit across all size grades (request a full-size set sample before bulk)
- Print placement and accuracy
- Wash test result (always wash before approving)

## Questions to Ask Before Choosing a Hoodie Manufacturer

1. What fleece GSM range can you source? Can I specify exact GSM and get a fabric certificate?
2. Do you do in-house embroidery and screen printing, or outsource?
3. What's your inline and final QC process? Do you provide AQL reports?
4. What's the sample lead time? How many revision rounds are included?
5. Can you show me hoodies you've produced for existing clients?
6. What's your on-time delivery rate for the last 12 months?
7. Do you provide fabric composition certificates?

## Finding a Hoodie Manufacturer vs. a T-Shirt Factory

Not all apparel manufacturers handle hoodies equally well. Hoodies require:
- Heavy-duty machinery for thick fleece
- Ribbing and waistband attachment expertise
- More complex pattern grading for oversized silhouettes
- Specific equipment for drawstring insertion

Ask for production photos of their hoodie operations specifically. A factory with strong t-shirt production doesn't automatically have great hoodie capability.
