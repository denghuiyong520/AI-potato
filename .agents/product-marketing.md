# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-07*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** OEM/ODM custom clothing manufacturer in China with low MOQ, fast sampling, and quality-controlled production for emerging brands.
**What it does:** Potato Apparel manufactures custom apparel (cut-and-sew OEM/ODM) for independent designers, e-commerce sellers, and growing labels. Clients send designs or tech packs; Potato handles fabric sourcing, sampling, bulk production, decoration (print/embroidery), QC, and worldwide shipping — turning a brand vision into finished, branded garments.
**Product category:** Custom clothing / apparel manufacturer (OEM/ODM). Customers search for "custom clothing manufacturer," "OEM clothing manufacturer," "low MOQ clothing manufacturer," "private label apparel."
**Product type:** B2B manufacturing service (made-to-order goods).
**Business model:** Per-order manufacturing. MOQ from 50 pcs (blanks) / 100 pcs (full custom OEM). Paid samples ($20–$80, refundable against first bulk order of 200+ pcs). Deposit + balance on production. Reported gross margin for clients 65–75% (vs ~65% print-on-demand).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Independent fashion brands, DTC/e-commerce sellers, Amazon FBA sellers, streetwear & activewear labels — typically early-stage to scaling (not yet large enough for huge factory MOQs, too big for print-on-demand economics).
**Decision-makers:** Brand founders, creative directors, e-commerce operators/owners. Usually a single founder-buyer (not a committee).
**Primary use case:** Get a custom-designed clothing line manufactured at an achievable MOQ, with reliable quality and on-time delivery.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Turn my designs into real, sellable branded garments without a 500+ unit minimum"
- "Find a factory that understands my aesthetic and communicates clearly"
- "Get samples fast so I can launch/iterate quickly"
**Use cases:**
- First-time founder launching an activewear/streetwear line
- E-commerce seller moving off print-on-demand to improve margins & branding
- Established label needing custom silhouettes, woven labels, custom packaging
- Amazon FBA seller needing FBA-prep + direct shipping

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Emerging brands can't get custom clothing made — traditional factories demand 500+ unit MOQs, while print-on-demand and blank wholesale don't allow true custom silhouettes, fabrics, or branding.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Print-on-demand (Printful): no custom patterns, no custom neck label/packaging, thinner margins, generic blanks
- Blank wholesale (Gildan/AS Colour): cheap but it's someone else's product — no custom fit, no AQL inspection
- Large traditional factories: MOQs too high, poor communication, slow/no sampling for small brands
**What it costs them:** Lost margin, inability to differentiate, stalled launches, wasted spend on samples that never match bulk.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of getting burned by an overseas factory (quality, communication, scams); anxiety about committing capital to a large order sight-unseen.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other China/Asia OEM/ODM apparel factories & sourcing agents — fall short on low-MOQ willingness, English communication, sampling speed, and transparent QC.
**Secondary:** Print-on-demand platforms (Printful, Printify) — fall short on customization, branding, and margin at scale.
**Indirect:** Blank wholesale (Gildan, AS Colour) + local screen printer — falls short on custom silhouettes, custom labels/packaging, and unified quality control.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- MOQ from 50 pcs (genuinely low for full cut-and-sew)
- 7–10 day sampling; samples credited against first bulk order
- In-house everything (cut, sew, print, embroider, QC, pack) — no outsourcing = consistent quality
- AQL 2.5 inspection with photo evidence on every order
- Custom silhouettes, woven neck labels, hang tags, retail packaging
- Amazon FBA prep + DDP worldwide shipping
**How we do it differently:** Single in-house facility (Guangzhou, ~15,000 m², 300+ workers, since 2014) handling the whole chain, paired with responsive 1-on-1 English communication.
**Why that's better:** Lower risk, faster launch, better margins, and a product that's genuinely the brand's own — not a relabeled blank.
**Why customers choose us:** Achievable MOQ + fast samples + reliable QC + clear communication, de-risking the leap to overseas manufacturing.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with print-on-demand margins/limits, or factories that ignore small brands or ghost on communication.
**Pull:** Low MOQ, fast samples, true custom branding, transparent QC.
**Habit:** Already running on Printful/blanks; inertia of a "good enough" setup.
**Anxiety:** Fear of quality mismatch, capital risk on first bulk order, language/timezone friction.
