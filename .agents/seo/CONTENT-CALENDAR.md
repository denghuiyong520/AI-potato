1. **Category landing pages first** (Pillar B) — highest commercial intent, reuse existing product data, biggest gap. ~13 pages.
2. **Comparison/BOFU pages** — capture buyers ready to switch.
3. **Fill TOFU gaps + link magnets** — fabric/GSM, calculators, glossary.
4. **Case studies & trust assets** — E-E-A-T.

## Production standards (every piece)

- Target one primary keyword + 2–3 secondary; put primary in title, H1, URL, first 100 words.
- 800–1,500 words for guides; 300–600 unique words for category pages.
- Original factory photos > stock wherever possible (E-E-A-T).
- Internal links: ≥1 to a category landing page, ≥1 to a related post, 1 CTA.
- Add appropriate schema (Article / FAQPage / CollectionPage).
- Write **English first and well**; only then translate category pages (reuse existing locale labels). Hold non-EN blog translation until EN ranks.
