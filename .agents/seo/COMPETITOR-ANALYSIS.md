## Key competitor: hongyuapparel.com

This is the **same manufacturing operation** (sister/source site). Treat as the benchmark and the cannibalization risk.
- **Opportunity:** Potato Apparel is the newer, cleaner, multilingual, better-structured site. With the technical SEO now ahead of most competitors, Potato can out-rank on long-tail + non-EN locales.
- **Risk:** two sites for one factory can split authority and create near-duplicate content. **Action:** keep messaging/positioning distinct (Potato = brand-builder partner for emerging labels; differentiate copy, don't copy-paste hongyuapparel content). Do not link-spam between them; one or two contextual links max.

## What the leaders do that we should copy

1. **Dedicated "custom [product] manufacturer" landing pages** (Apliiq, Bryden) — not just filtered catalog views. Each has unique copy, FAQ, pricing guidance, MOQ table, gallery. → **Our biggest gap.**
2. **Deep buying guides** with original data/photos (we have 14 posts — good start; need more original factory photography + data).
3. **Trust stack:** certifications, factory audit badges, real client logos/case studies, named QC standards. → Build our E-E-A-T.
4. **Comparison/alternative pages** ("Printful alternative", "vs" pages) that capture BOFU traffic.
5. **Free tools / calculators** (cost calculator, MOQ calculator, GSM guide) — strong link magnets in this niche.

## Keyword gaps (opportunity — low competition, high relevance)

- "low MOQ [hoodie/t-shirt/activewear] manufacturer" (long-tail, winnable early)
- "clothing manufacturer for startups / small brands"
- "custom [Y2K / vintage wash / oversized boxy] t-shirt manufacturer" (trend terms, less contested)
- "cut and sew manufacturer [country] for [niche]"
- "[fabric] gsm guide", "tech pack template", "MOQ explained" (TOFU link magnets)
- Non-EN: "fabricant de vêtements personnalisés", "Bekleidungshersteller kleine Auflage", "fabricante de ropa personalizada MOQ bajo" — competitors are weaker in DE/ES.

## Takeaway

Potato Apparel's **technical foundation now beats most competitors**. The race is won on (1) turning category pages into real landing pages, (2) E-E-A-T/trust depth, and (3) long-tail content velocity — not on chasing head terms early.
